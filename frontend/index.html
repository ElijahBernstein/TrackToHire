How Gmail updates work
  1. 1
    Read securely

    TrackToHire temporarily reads the full email. Full message bodies are not saved.

  2. 2
    Understand and verify

    AI identifies the current outcome and must provide exact evidence. Unrelated mail is hidden and uncertainty stays Unknown.

  3. 3
    Match and approve

    Strong matches are selected automatically. Otherwise, you choose the application before confirming the change.
